Nice to meet you!

Creative in ideas, rigorous in execution.

My name is Emilie and I'm a second-year student in the BUT MMI (multimedia and web) programme at the IUT du Limousin, in Limoges, France. I love it when a design is beautiful and everything is in its place: a clean grid, a clear hierarchy, carefully chosen colours.

I work from research to mockup, then all the way to integration, so that what was designed is really what goes live.

And when I close Figma: Zelda, Persona or Valorant, pop and rock in my headphones, and a soft spot for hardware.
