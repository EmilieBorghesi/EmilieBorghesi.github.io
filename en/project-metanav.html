MetaNav a mobile app inspired by Persona 5 Royal

Mobile app designed for Queen, the Phantom Thieves' strategist in Persona 5 Royal: Palace search, map, alerts and team chat.

The project in short

Role: UX/UI designer · Tool: Figma · Fictional university project

Brief
Imagine the app a fictional character would use if they had a smartphone: a real service meeting an everyday need, using the work's visual identity, world and assets. I chose Persona 5 Royal and Makoto Niijima, codename Queen, the Phantom Thieves' strategist. MetaNav does exist in the game, but its interface is never shown, so it was all mine to imagine.
Process
I designed MetaNav as Queen's work tool for planning infiltrations: a keyword search for Palaces (name, location, distortion), a map to follow the exploration, a history of Palaces already visited, alerts with the countdown to Joker's expulsion, and a team chat. For the interface, I used the game's codes: red and black, slanted shapes, cut-out lettering for titles, speech bubbles and character portraits.
Result
Nine consistent mobile screens, from login to profile: home, search, Palace navigation, alerts, three chats and profile, linked by a shared navigation bar.
