Graphic design October 2025

Stickers six stickers about my skills

Six stickers drawn in Illustrator to illustrate my technical and people skills, designed for screen and print alike.

The project in short

Role: graphic designer · Tool: Illustrator · University project · 1 week

Brief
The brief: create a series of stickers about my technical skills (hard skills) and my personal qualities (soft skills), designed from the start for two uses, print and digital.
Process
In one week, I turned each skill into an image in Illustrator: two patterns for my software, Illustrator and Premiere Pro; “Organisation” as a maze and “Teamwork makes the dream work” in neon for my qualities; a self-portrait and my nickname in lettering to sign the series. Simple shapes and flat colours to stay readable at a very small size.
Result
Six vector stickers that sum up my profile at a glance, as readable in a messaging app as stuck on a laptop.
