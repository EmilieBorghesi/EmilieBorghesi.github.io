My projects

Each project is told in three parts: the brief, my process, then the result.

01 · UX/UI

Neko café landing page for a fictional coffee shop

Role: UX/UI designer · Tool: Figma · Fictional project

Brief
Neko café is an imaginary tea room, halfway between a Japanese kissaten and a French pâtisserie. It needed a home page that makes you want to walk in and says the essentials: the concept, the menu, the address.
Process
I split the page into three parts — concept, menu, where to find us — then built a soft world in Figma: cloud shapes, a coral, cream and matcha green palette, headings doubled in Japanese, and product cards with photo and price.
Result
A complete landing page mockup, from header to footer: the story of the place, seven signature products, a button to the full menu and a location map.

02 · Graphic design

Stickers six stickers about my skills

Role: graphic designer · Tool: Illustrator · University project · 1 week

Brief
The brief: create a series of stickers about my technical skills (hard skills) and my personal qualities (soft skills), designed from the start for two uses, print and digital.
Process
In one week, I turned each skill into an image in Illustrator: two patterns for my software, Illustrator and Premiere Pro; “Organisation” as a maze and “Teamwork makes the dream work” in neon for my qualities; a self-portrait and my nickname in lettering to sign the series. Simple shapes and flat colours to stay readable at a very small size.
Result
Six vector stickers that sum up my profile at a glance, as readable in a messaging app as stuck on a laptop.

03 · Web

Film catalogue a database-driven website

Role: front-end and back-end development · Tools: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP · University project (SAé 2.03) · 3 weeks

Brief
The question: how can people browse content stored in a database smoothly, and interact with it? The site had to be clear and easy to use, with a catalogue that is simple to navigate.
Process
In three weeks, I built a film catalogue in the style of a streaming platform: profiles, films sorted by category, a detail page and favourites, plus a protected admin area to add new films to the database. Front end in HTML, CSS (BEM) and JavaScript, back end in PHP connected to the database, then deployment on my own hosting.
Result
A working website, live online: each profile finds its favourites and only sees films suited to its age, all loaded from the database without changing page.
