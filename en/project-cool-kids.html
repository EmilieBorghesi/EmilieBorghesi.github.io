Emilie and the Cool Kids a WordPress click & collect website

A WordPress click & collect website for Emilie and the Cool Kids: menu, product pages, cart, checkout and in-store pickup tracking.

The project in short

Role: UX design and integration · Tools: WordPress, WooCommerce · University project, Digital Creation track

Brief
Create a click & collect website for a restaurant brand that doesn't offer the service yet, while following its whole visual identity: logo, colours, typefaces, tone and photos. The question: how do you build a website that offers a quality user experience? I chose Emilie and the Cool Kids, the cookie and bagel coffee shop.
Process
I started by analysing the specifications and defining the user journeys, from browsing the menu to collecting the order in store, then made an interactive mockup. I then built the site with WordPress and WooCommerce: a menu sorted into savoury, sweet and drinks, a shop filtered by time of day, product pages with options and allergens, a customer account, cart, checkout and order tracking. The brand's identity runs throughout, from the night purple to the product illustrations, with special care for the mobile layout.
Result
A complete, working e-commerce site: customers fill their cart, choose free in-store pickup, pay by bank transfer or on collection, then follow their order in three steps, from “Order received” to “Ready for pickup”.
